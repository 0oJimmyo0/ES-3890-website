Tell me about TRACE.

SHORT ANSWER

TRACE is an autoregressive EEG foundation model developed at Yale’s Clinic NLP Lab. It learns representations across heterogeneous montages and recording settings, using temporal routing and cross‑channel expert sharing. The project collects, cleans, and preprocesses multi‑source EEG data, implements baseline models, and evaluates downstream performance. The manuscript “TRACE: Temporal Routing with Autoregressive Cross‑channel Experts for EEG Representation Learning” is under review for NeurIPS 2026.
