Tell me about TRACE.

TRACE is an autoregressive EEG foundation model project focused on representation learning across heterogeneous montages and recording settings. The research investigates temporal routing and cross‑channel expert sharing to enable transferable EEG representations. Mingyang contributed by collecting, cleaning, and preprocessing multi‑source EEG data, implementing baseline foundation models, and evaluating downstream performance. The project is currently under review. The related publication, “TRACE: Temporal Routing with Autoregressive Cross‑channel Experts for EEG Representation Learning,” is also under review and was submitted to NeurIPS 2026.
